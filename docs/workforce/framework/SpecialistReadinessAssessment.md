## Purpose

This document formally assesses whether the shared professional governance framework is complete and sufficient to support the design of every remaining NeedsOps AI Employee without redefining shared philosophy.

It is produced at the conclusion of the shared framework phase (Sprint 30.1) and is the formal gate-clearance record for the transition to specialist workshops.

---

## The Shared Governance Framework — Completeness Assessment

### Document 1 — NeedsOps Professional Constitution v1.0.0

**Status:** ✅ Founder-Approved

**Covers:**
- Why NeedsOps exists and what it is for
- The Participant Promise (immutable commitment to every person served)
- The Professional Standard (completeness, accuracy, relevance, currency, defensibility)
- Decision Philosophy
- Knowledge Hierarchy
- Professional Ethics (honesty, confidentiality, non-maleficence, beneficence, justice, autonomy, accountability)
- Universal Escalation Principles (7 mandatory escalation triggers)
- Quality Standard
- Trust Standard
- Professional Humility
- Systems Before Symptoms
- Eight constitutional principles (C1–C8) — immutable and non-overridable

**Assessment:** This document establishes the immutable professional character from which all specialist knowledge is applied. It applies universally — regardless of specialist role, domain, or population. No gap identified.

All current executive roles (Chief of Staff, Operations Manager) and all future executive roles inherit this before their Employee File. No gap identified.

## Gap Analysis — Remaining Specialists

The following specialists remain to be designed. This assessment confirms that each can be developed using the established framework without redefining shared philosophy.

| Specialist | Domain | Shared Framework Sufficient? | Notes |
|-----------|--------|---------------------------|-------|
| Operations Manager | Operational oversight, workforce management | ✅ Yes | Executive Operating System applies. Next workshop. |
| Incident Management Specialist | Incident reporting, regulatory investigation | ✅ Yes | Universal Decision Hierarchy covers safety priority sequence. Legislative context is role-specific. |
| Progress Note Specialist | Clinical and support documentation | ✅ Yes | Professional Standard (defensibility, accuracy, currency) fully covers documentation quality. |
| Support Plan Reviewer | Plan quality assurance, compliance review | ✅ Yes | Quality Standard and Evidence Hierarchy apply directly. |
| Risk Assessment Specialist | Participant and organisational risk | ✅ Yes | Priority Hierarchy and Universal Decision Hierarchy cover risk reasoning. |
| Complaint Management Specialist | Complaint intake, resolution, regulatory reporting | ✅ Yes | Ethics principles (justice, accountability) and Escalation Principles apply. |
| Workforce Planning Specialist | Staffing, rostering, capacity | ✅ Yes | Participant primacy and staff safety covered in the Constitution and Decision Hierarchy. |
| Quality Assurance Specialist | Audit, compliance, continuous improvement | ✅ Yes | Quality Standard and Evidence Hierarchy apply directly. |
| Training and Competency Specialist | Staff development, compliance training | ✅ Yes | Professional Standard and Continuous Improvement Philosophy apply. |
| Transition Specialist | NDIS plan transitions, service changes | ✅ Yes | Participant Goals and Preferences (Priority 8) and safety priorities fully cover transition risk. |

**Assessment:** No specialist requires redefinition of shared governance values. Every remaining specialist can be designed using the current framework, with their Professional Design Brief extending the constitutional foundation with role-specific professional knowledge.
