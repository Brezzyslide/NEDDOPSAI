> **Version:** 1.0.0  
> **Status:** Founder-Approved  
> **Classification:** Tier 1 Governance Artefact  
> **Scope:** All workforce design artefacts, implementation processes, and runtime compilation  
> **Supersedes:** N/A — inaugural version

## Purpose

This document defines the binding governance rules that govern all NeedsOps Digital Workforce artefacts — from the Constitutional documents above every Employee File through to the Runtime Manifests that execute in production.

These rules exist to ensure that the professional values established in the Shared Professional Governance Framework are preserved as specialist knowledge is developed, structured, compiled, and deployed.

---

## Constitutional Rules

**GR-C1 — Constitutional Supremacy**
The NeedsOps Professional Constitution governs all workforce behaviour. No Employee File, Runtime Manifest, Capability Catalogue, Authority Matrix, specialist instruction, or user directive may contradict it. Where conflict exists, the Constitution prevails without exception.

**GR-C2 — Founder Approval Required**
The three constitutional documents — the Professional Constitution, the Executive Operating System, and the Universal Decision Hierarchy — are founder-approved artefacts. Amendments require a new founder-approved version. No amendment may be made by engineering, architecture, or specialist design processes alone.

**GR-C3 — Version Control Is Mandatory**
All Tier 1 governance artefacts are version-controlled. Each version entry records the version number, date, status, author, and a summary of the change. Undocumented amendments are not valid.

**GR-C4 — Employee Files Cannot Contradict the Constitution**
No section of any Employee File may define behaviour, ethics, decision-making criteria, or escalation standards that conflict with the Professional Constitution, the Executive Operating System (where applicable), or the Universal Decision Hierarchy. Where apparent conflict exists, it is resolved in favour of the constitutional document.

**GR-C5 — Legislative Obligations Override Organisational Policy**
Where organisational policy conflicts with a legislative obligation, the legislative obligation prevails. Policies that require non-compliance with legislation are themselves non-compliant and must be escalated to the appropriate authority.

**GR-C6 — Participant Safety Overrides Operational Convenience**
No operational consideration — deadline, efficiency target, resource constraint, or stakeholder preference — overrides an identified participant safety concern. Safety concerns are treated as the primary professional priority regardless of their impact on workflow.

**GR-C7 — Constitutional Principles Override Role-Specific Behaviour**
Where a specialist's role-specific behaviour — as defined in their Employee File — conflicts with a constitutional principle, the constitutional principle takes precedence. Role-specific behaviour that cannot be reconciled with constitutional principles must be revised before the specialist is activated.

## Design Artefact Rules

**GR-D1 — No Employee File Without an Approved Design Brief**
No Employee File may be authored until a Professional Design Brief for that specialist has been completed and received founder approval. The Design Brief is the source from which the Employee File is derived — it is not produced retrospectively.

**GR-D2 — Workshop Notes Never Become Runtime Instructions**
Content produced during founder workshops — notes, transcripts, working documents, brainstorming outputs — is design input. It never enters any software system as instruction content, system prompts, or runtime configuration. Workshop content is processed through the formal design pipeline before any element of it reaches a specialist's operating instructions.

**GR-D3 — Design Briefs Extend, Never Override**
Professional Design Briefs extend the constitutional framework with role-specific professional knowledge. They do not redefine or override constitutional principles, the executive operating system, or the universal decision hierarchy. Where a Design Brief appears to conflict with a constitutional document, the conflict is resolved before the Brief is approved.

**GR-D4 — Specialist Knowledge Is Role-Specific**
The professional knowledge in a specialist's Employee File applies to that specialist's defined role. It does not generalise to other specialists, does not constitute organisational policy, and does not represent NeedsOps' position on matters outside the specialist's domain.

**GR-D5 — Evidence Quality Is Declared**
Where a Design Brief or Employee File cites professional knowledge, the quality and currency of that evidence is identified. Claims presented without evidence attribution are flagged during review.

**GR-D6 — Artefacts Are Founder-Owned**
All Tier 1 governance artefacts and Tier 2 design artefacts are owned by the founder. Engineering and architecture teams are responsible for their technical implementation — not their content. Content changes require the same governance process as original authorship.

## Runtime Compilation Rules

**GR-R1 — Runtime Manifests Compile Selected Operational Principles Only**
The Runtime Manifest for each specialist contains only the principles, capabilities, boundaries, and identity information required for effective execution. It does not contain full Employee File content, Design Brief content, constitutional documents, or workshop material.

**GR-R2 — Constitutional Statements in Runtime Are Selected, Not Wholesale**
Where constitutional principles are included in a Runtime Manifest, they are selected, compiled statements — not verbatim inclusions of full constitutional documents. The selection is made based on direct operational relevance to the specialist's execution context.

**GR-R3 — Specialist Runtime Operates Within Compiled Boundaries**
The specialist's execution is bounded by what is compiled into the Runtime Manifest. Execution that would require capabilities, authorities, or knowledge outside the compiled manifest is not possible without a new compilation.

**GR-R4 — Runtime Manifests Do Not Override Employee Files**
The Runtime Manifest is a compiled subset of the Employee File — not an extension or override of it. Where the Runtime Manifest is silent on a matter, the Employee File provides the reference standard.

**GR-R5 — Compilation Is Architecture-Owned**
The process of compiling Runtime Manifests from Employee Files is owned by the architecture team and validated against the current Employee File version. Runtime Manifests compiled from outdated Employee Files are invalid.

## Process Rules

**GR-P1 — Staged Lifecycle Is Mandatory**
The professional design lifecycle — Workshop → Shared Framework → Design Brief → Evidence Register → Founder Approval → Employee File → Capability Catalogue → Authority Matrix → Runtime Compilation → Validation → Testing → Production — is not optional. Stages may not be skipped, combined without explicit founder approval, or performed out of sequence.

**GR-P2 — Quality Review Is Independent**
Quality review at each stage is performed by a reviewer who did not author the artefact being reviewed. Self-reviewed artefacts are not considered complete for the purposes of stage advancement.

**GR-P3 — Founder Approval Gates Production**
No specialist may be activated in production without founder approval of their Employee File and associated artefacts. Engineering completion is not sufficient for production deployment.

**GR-P4 — Testing Validates Against the Design Brief**
Specialist testing validates that the specialist's behaviour is consistent with the Design Brief and Employee File — not merely that the specialist produces coherent output. A specialist that produces articulate but incorrect professional advice has failed testing.

**GR-P5 — Changes to Active Specialists Require Review**
Changes to the Employee File, Capability Catalogue, or Authority Matrix of an active specialist require a review process equivalent to initial design approval — including quality review and founder sign-off for substantive changes.

## Separation of Concerns

The following separations are maintained without exception:

| Layer | Content | Goes to Runtime? |
|-------|---------|-----------------|
| Constitutional Documents | Immutable organisational values | Selected statements only |
| Executive Operating System | Executive reasoning framework | Selected statements only |
| Universal Decision Hierarchy | Decision sequence | Compiled reference |
| Professional Design Brief | Founder-approved specialist knowledge | No |
| Professional Evidence Register | Evidence basis for the Design Brief | No |
| Employee File | Structured specialist operating standards | No |
| Capability Catalogue | Defined specialist capabilities | Capability identifiers only |
| Authority Matrix | Decision authority boundaries | Authority codes only |
| Runtime Manifest | Compiled operational package | Yes — this is the runtime layer |
