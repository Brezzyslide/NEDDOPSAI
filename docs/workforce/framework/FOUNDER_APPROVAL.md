## Purpose

This register is the formal governance trail for all founder-approved constitutional artefacts in the NeedsOps Digital Workforce.

It records what has been approved, when, by whom, at which version, and what changed between versions. It provides the audit evidence that the Digital Workforce is built on deliberate, documented, and founder-approved principles — not informally evolved assumptions.

This record is required for:

- Enterprise governance and due diligence reviews
- Regulatory audits involving the NeedsOps AI workforce
- Onboarding of organisational customers who require evidence of governance maturity
- Internal review processes when constitutional documents are proposed for amendment
- Demonstrating that specialist behaviour is traceable to explicit founder intent

## Review Schedule

### Mandatory review triggers

A review of one or more constitutional artefacts is mandatory when any of the following occur:

| Trigger | Artefacts to review |
|---------|-------------------|
| Material change to applicable legislation (e.g. NDIS Act, Aged Care Act, Privacy Act) | Universal Decision Hierarchy; Professional Constitution (Ethics, Escalation) |
| Material change to applicable regulation or practice standards | Universal Decision Hierarchy; Governance Rules |
| Material change to organisational philosophy or mission | Professional Constitution; Executive Operating System |
| Addition of a new professional domain not previously contemplated | Universal Decision Hierarchy; Professional Constitution |
| Identification of a gap or conflict in the constitutional framework | The affected document(s) |
| Annual scheduled review | All documents |

### Annual review

All constitutional artefacts are reviewed annually. The review confirms:
- Documents remain consistent with the current legislative and regulatory environment
- Documents remain consistent with the founder's professional philosophy
- No conflicts have emerged between constitutional documents and implemented specialist behaviour
- Any proposed amendments are assessed and either approved or deferred

The annual review is scheduled each August.

---

## Amendment Process

Amendments to any document in this register require the following process:

1. **Identification** — Architecture or the founder identifies a proposed change and documents the rationale
2. **Draft** — Architecture prepares a revised version with tracked changes
3. **Review** — Subject Matter Expert (where applicable) reviews for professional accuracy
4. **Constitutional conflict check** — Architecture confirms the amendment does not create conflict with other constitutional documents
5. **Founder approval** — The founder reviews and approves the revised version
6. **Version increment** — A new version entry is added to the document's version history and this register's change log
7. **Impact assessment** — Architecture assesses whether any active specialist's Employee File or Runtime Manifest requires update in response to the amendment
8. **Propagation** — Any required updates to Employee Files, Capability Catalogues, or Runtime Manifests are completed and validated before the amended document is considered fully in effect

No amendment takes effect until Steps 5–8 are complete.
