SPRINT 29K.4.1 — SEMANTIC ENTAILMENT & ABSENCE-CONTRADICTION HARDENING GATE

This is a narrow proof/hardening sprint following Sprint 29K.4.

DO NOT build the Evidence UI.
DO NOT redesign the claim architecture.
DO NOT redesign KRS.
DO NOT introduce broad new functionality.
DO NOT weaken any Sprint 29J, 29K.2, 29K.3 or 29K.4 guarantees.

The purpose of this gate is to investigate and close, or precisely bound, TWO residual integrity risks exposed by Sprint 29K.4:

RISK 1 — POSITIVE CLAIM SEMANTIC SUPPORT

A passage can be genuine, contain the same words/numbers/actors, and still not support the proposition made by the claim.

Example:

Evidence:
“The Complaints Officer must acknowledge complaints within five business days.”

Claim:
“The Complaints Officer must resolve complaints within five business days.”

Exact span verification can succeed.
Actor matches.
Timeframe matches.
Obligation matches.
Negation matches.

But the claim is NOT supported because acknowledge ≠ resolve.

RISK 2 — ABSENCE CONTRADICTION SEMANTICS

A targeted absence search can retrieve a highly relevant passage without that passage proving the supposedly absent requirement actually exists.

Example absence claim:
“The policy does not define an escalation procedure.”

Retrieved passage:
“An escalation procedure is currently under development.”

This is highly relevant to the search query.

But it SUPPORTS the absence finding rather than contradicting it.

A high retrieval relevance score must therefore NOT automatically produce contradicted_absence.

CORE PRINCIPLE:

RELEVANCE ≠ ENTAILMENT.

A real quotation does not automatically support a claim.

A relevant search result does not automatically contradict an absence claim.

PART B — REMOVE THE WEAK L1 ACCEPTANCE CONDITION
========================================================

Sprint 29K.4 documented an adversarial L1 case where:

expect(["supporting","uncertain"]).toContain(result.classification)

was accepted.

This is NOT sufficient as an integrity proof.

For a deliberately semantically mismatched proposition, "supporting" must not be an acceptable passing result.

Replace weak tests with explicit expected outcomes.

For known semantic substitutions such as:

acknowledge → resolve

the result must NOT be "supporting".

Expected minimum result:

support_uncertain

or a stronger contradictory/unsupported state if the architecture supports it.

No adversarial test may pass merely because either the safe OR unsafe result occurred.

========================================================

Investigate whether deterministic validation can be strengthened by representing the material proposition of a claim.

At minimum consider:

SUBJECT / ACTOR
Who performs the action?

ACTION
What is actually being done?
Examples:
- acknowledge
- investigate
- resolve
- escalate
- approve
- review
- retain
- notify
- record
- report

OBJECT
What is the action applied to?

OBLIGATION
- must
- shall
- required
- should
- may
- can
- prohibited

NEGATION
Does / does not.
Must / must not.

QUANTITY / TIMEFRAME
Numbers, periods, thresholds, percentages.

CONDITION
Under what circumstances does the rule apply?

SCOPE
Which complaints, employees, records, departments, etc.

Do NOT build a general natural-language understanding engine.

========================================================
PART D — ACTION / PREDICATE CONFLICT
========================================================

Add a deterministic action/predicate integrity check if technically viable.

Examples that MUST NOT be treated as equivalent:

acknowledge ≠ resolve
investigate ≠ resolve
review ≠ approve
recommend ≠ require
record ≠ report
notify ≠ escalate
retain ≠ archive/delete
consult ≠ obtain approval

Be careful with legitimate equivalents/synonyms.

Examples that MAY be equivalent depending on wording:

acknowledge ≈ confirm receipt
record ≈ log
retain ≈ keep
notify ≈ inform

Do not create an enormous uncontrolled synonym dictionary.

Use a bounded domain vocabulary suitable for NeedsOps operational/compliance work.

If the validator cannot determine whether two actions are equivalent, classify support as uncertain rather than supporting.

SAFE DEFAULT:

UNKNOWN SEMANTIC RELATIONSHIP → UNCERTAIN

not:

UNKNOWN SEMANTIC RELATIONSHIP → SUPPORTING

========================================================
PART E — ENTAILMENT DECISION LADDER
========================================================

Rework or extend semantic support classification around a conservative decision ladder.

Conceptually:

1. Is the supporting span genuine?
   NO → invalid_binding

2. Are there deterministic material conflicts?
   YES → support_uncertain or contradictory

3. Does the evidence contain the same material action/proposition?
   YES → continue

4. Are actor, obligation, negation, timeframe, quantity and scope consistent?
   NO → support_uncertain / contradictory

5. Can semantic support be established confidently?
   YES → supporting

6. Is semantic relationship unresolved?
   YES → uncertain

Unknown must not default to supporting.

Do not downgrade obviously valid claims merely because the wording is not identical.

The purpose is conservative evidence integrity, not literal string matching.

========================================================
PART F — DECIDE WHETHER A BOUNDED SEMANTIC VERIFIER IS NECESSARY
========================================================

After strengthening deterministic checks, run the adversarial suite.

ONLY THEN determine whether a bounded semantic verifier is needed.

Do not introduce an unrestricted second LLM automatically.

If deterministic validation cannot reliably distinguish material propositions, investigate a bounded semantic validation stage.

If a model-based verifier is proposed or implemented, it MUST:

- receive only:
  - claim text
  - claim type
  - supporting passage(s)
  - source metadata required for context
- NOT receive hidden prompts
- NOT receive specialist chain-of-thought
- NOT rewrite the claim
- NOT rewrite Completed Work
- NOT generate new evidence
- NOT perform retrieval
- NOT use training knowledge as evidence
- return only a constrained classification such as:

  supported
  unsupported
  uncertain
  contradictory

plus short machine-readable reason codes.

Its result must be a validation signal.

It must NEVER manufacture evidence.

Failure/timeout must result in uncertain, never supported.

If no model-based verifier is implemented, explicitly demonstrate why deterministic validation is sufficient for the tested operational domain.

========================================================
PART G — ABSENCE CANDIDATE CLASSIFICATION
========================================================

Fix the assumption:

candidate relevance >= threshold
    therefore
contradicted_absence

This is unsafe.

A targeted absence search result must first be classified.

At minimum distinguish:

1. REQUIREMENT_PRESENT
The passage positively establishes the supposedly absent requirement.

Example:
“High-severity complaints must be escalated to the Head of Operations within two business days.”

For absence claim:
“No escalation timeframe is defined.”

→ contradicted_absence

2. REQUIREMENT_ABSENT_OR_PENDING
The passage discusses the concept but confirms it is missing, undefined, pending or under development.

Example:
“An escalation procedure is currently under development.”

→ this does NOT contradict the absence claim.

3. CONTEXT_ONLY
The passage discusses escalation but does not establish whether the specific requirement exists.

Example:
“Escalated complaints are reviewed at management meetings.”

For:
“No escalation timeframe is defined.”

This proves escalation exists but does NOT prove a timeframe exists.

→ absence remains unverified unless the full search satisfies absence-proof conditions.

4. AMBIGUOUS
Meaning cannot be safely classified.

→ unverified_absence

A relevant candidate must never automatically equal REQUIREMENT_PRESENT.

========================================================
PART H — ABSENCE CLAIM COMPONENT MATCHING
========================================================

Absence verification must verify the actual missing ELEMENT.

Example 1:

Claim:
“No escalation timeframe is defined.”

Evidence:
“Complaints may be escalated to the Service Manager.”

This establishes escalation and ownership.

It does NOT establish a timeframe.

Therefore:
NOT contradicted_absence for the timeframe claim.

Example 2:

Claim:
“No escalation owner is defined.”

Evidence:
“Complaints must be escalated within two days.”

This establishes a timeframe.

It does NOT establish ownership.

Therefore:
NOT contradicted_absence for the owner claim.

Example 3:

Claim:
“No escalation owner is defined.”

Evidence:
“The Head of Operations decides whether a complaint is escalated.”

This DOES establish ownership.

→ contradicted_absence.

The verifier must compare the missing proposition against what the candidate passage actually establishes.
