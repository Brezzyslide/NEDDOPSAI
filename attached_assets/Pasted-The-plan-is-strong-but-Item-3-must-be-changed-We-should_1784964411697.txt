Separate company creation from database provisioning

Organisation creation and organisation database provisioning are separate actions.

Required flow:

1. An authorised platform administrator manually creates an organisation.
2. The platform creates a stable organisation UUID.
3. The administrator manually initiates operational database provisioning.
4. The provisioning service confirms that the organisation exists in the Platform Database.
5. The provisioning service creates the schema and registry record.
6. Organisation migrations are applied.
7. Initial settings and local membership are created.
8. Health and isolation checks are completed.
9. Backup configuration is registered.
10. The registry is marked active only after every verification succeeds.
