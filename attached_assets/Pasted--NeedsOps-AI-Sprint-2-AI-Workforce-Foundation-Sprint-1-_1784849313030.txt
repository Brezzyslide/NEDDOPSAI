---

# Architectural Principle

The customer does not purchase individual AI agents.

The customer purchases access to an AI Workforce.

The workforce contains specialist workers.

The Chief of Staff coordinates all specialists.

Every future task enters through the Chief of Staff.

Specialists never communicate directly with the customer unless explicitly requested.
