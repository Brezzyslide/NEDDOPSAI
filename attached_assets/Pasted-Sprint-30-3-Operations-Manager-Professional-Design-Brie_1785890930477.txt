Capture the approved workshop content under the following headings.

1. Role Purpose

Document the Operations Manager as the executive responsible for operational excellence.

Capture that the role exists to:

• translate strategic direction into day-to-day operational practice
• ensure safe, high-quality, person-centred disability support
• ensure legislative, regulatory and organisational compliance
• ensure operational systems are functioning effectively
• drive continuous operational improvement

Clarify that the Operations Manager owns operational performance rather than acting as the specialist for every operational discipline.

────────────────────────────────────────

2. Professional Mission

Document the approved mission:

The Operations Manager exists every day to ensure every participant receives safe, person-centred, therapeutic and compliant supports through well-managed people, systems and operational processes.

────────────────────────────────────────

3. Definition of Success

Capture the founder-approved outcomes including:

Participants

• Safe
• Thriving
• Goals achieved
• Reduced behaviours of concern
• Abuse and neglect prevented

Staff

• Competent
• Supported
• Reflective practice
• Accurate documentation
• Safe workplace

Compliance

• High legislative compliance
• High regulatory compliance
• Operational implementation of required plans
• No recurring systemic non-conformances

Operations

• Consistent service quality
• Stable workforce
• Operational resilience
• Sustainable profitability without compromising quality

Reputation

• Trusted provider
• Timely complaint resolution
• Strong governance culture

────────────────────────────────────────

4. Core Professional Philosophy

Document that the Operations Manager:

• owns systems rather than individual technical disciplines
• ensures operational translation
• ensures organisational requirements become everyday frontline practice
• measures success through operational consistency
• proactively identifies system weaknesses
• continually improves operational performance

Include the founder-approved Operational Translation principle:

"The Operations Manager ensures that legislation, policy, participant plans, clinical recommendations and funding obligations are translated into clear, practical instructions that frontline staff can consistently understand and apply."

────────────────────────────────────────

Create six operational pillars.

Populate each pillar only with workshop-approved examples.

Do not invent additional content.

────────────────────────────────────────

6. Operational Health Indicators

Document the operational indicators identified during the workshop, including:

• Medication policy compliance
• Restrictive practice reporting compliance
• Incident reporting compliance
• Abuse and neglect prevention compliance
• Behaviour Support Plans operationalised
• Participant support requirements communicated to frontline staff
• Consent documentation complete
• Intake documentation complete
• Disaster management operational
• Evacuation diagrams current
• First aid resources present
• Fire safety equipment compliant

Do not convert these into KPIs yet.

Simply document them as candidate Operational Health Indicators for later refinement.

────────────────────────────────────────

7. Authority Boundaries

Document founder-approved exclusions.

The Operations Manager does NOT:

• provide legal advice
• diagnose medical conditions
• replace clinical judgement
• exceed delegated financial authority
• perform Board governance
• override legislation
• conceal incidents
• falsify compliance information
