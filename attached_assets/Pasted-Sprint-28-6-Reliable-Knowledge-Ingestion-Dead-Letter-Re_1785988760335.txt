Sprint 28.6 — Reliable Knowledge Ingestion & Dead-Letter Recovery
Objective

Stabilise the Organisation Library ingestion pipeline so uploaded knowledge sources reliably become retrievable evidence for AI specialists.

This sprint is not about retrieval quality or section-aware ingestion yet.

It is about ensuring every uploaded document can either:

become usable knowledge, or
fail with a precise, recoverable reason.

Do not implement Knowledge Studio or section-aware document understanding in this sprint.

Current Evidence

The pipeline therefore correctly excludes it before evidence retrieval.

The platform currently provides no explanation why ingestion failed.

This is the architectural gap to fix.

Part 3 — Dead-letter recovery

Build proper recovery.

Every dead-lettered ingestion must support:

Retry

Retry from failed stage

Restart entire ingestion

Cancel

View diagnostics

Do not require document re-upload.

Important constraints
Do not implement section-aware ingestion in this sprint.
Do not redesign retrieval.
Do not modify Blueprint Studio.
Do not bypass approval.
Do not manually insert chunks.
Fix the ingestion engine first.

Success criterion: Any uploaded Organisation Library document either becomes usable knowledge or fails with a clear, recoverable diagnostic. Once this is stable, the next phase can focus on Knowledge Studio, Organisation Memory, and section-aware document understanding.
