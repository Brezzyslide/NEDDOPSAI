Sprint 27.3 – Knowledge Resolution & Evidence Delivery Engine

This sprint implements the missing architecture between the Knowledge Library and specialist execution.

Background

The inspection identified three architectural gaps:

1. Specialists receive only metadata (title, sourceType) for organisation documents. They never receive the document content or retrieved evidence.

2. Replacing a document resets the knowledge source to status='uploaded', making it disappear from the execution pipeline until manually re-approved.

3. Blueprint selection relies primarily on keyword matching, causing valid operational requests to fall back to the Chief of Staff instead of the appropriate specialist.

These are architecture gaps, not isolated bugs.

Do not weaken validation.
Do not bypass evidence requirements.
Do not remove blueprint validation.

Instead complete the knowledge delivery architecture.

PART 5 – Document Replacement

Review:

replaceSourceVersion()

Current behaviour:

status='approved'

↓

replace

↓

status='uploaded'

↓

document disappears

Required:

Replacing a version must preserve the parent knowledge source approval.

Update only:

- storage key
- checksum
- version label
- timestamps

Do not reset approval unless explicitly required.

Immediately enqueue:

- ingestion
- embedding generation
- evidence refresh

Do not require manual intervention.
