One refinement I’d add

At the end of Sprint 23, don’t immediately move to OCR or cloud connectors. Instead, pause for a Version 1 Readiness Review. Assess whether a new customer can complete the full journey:

1. Sign up.
2. Create an organisation.
3. Select an AI Workforce.
4. Connect the desktop app (or use the web).
5. Upload organisational knowledge.
6. Train specialists.
7. Ask the Chief of Staff to perform work.
8. Review and approve the output.
9. Retrieve completed work later.

If that entire journey feels polished and intuitive, then you’re ready to move into the next phase of expanding knowledge sources and enterprise integrations. That checkpoint will likely deliver more value than immediately adding more backend capabilities.
