Sprint 30.2 – Specialist Development Workspace

Objective

The shared governance framework is now complete and founder approved.

Before creating any additional specialists, establish the standard workspace that every current and future NeedsOps AI employee will use throughout its lifecycle.

This sprint creates the development structure only.

Do NOT create specialist content.

Do NOT generate Employee Files.

Do NOT generate Design Briefs.

Do NOT modify runtime behaviour.

Do NOT implement specialist logic.

Do NOT generate prompts.

The objective is to prepare a repeatable, version-controlled development environment for every specialist.

Background

The following constitutional artefacts are complete:

• Shared Professional Constitution
• Executive Operating System
• Universal Decision Hierarchy
• Governance Rules
• Professional Design Lifecycle
• Founder Approval Register

Every specialist will now be built from these shared artefacts.
