# NeedsOps AI+

# Sprint 10 — Digital Workforce Intelligence & Execution

Continue from Sprint 9.7.

Do **not** build additional Platform Console features unless they are required to support workforce execution.

The objective of Sprint 10 is to transform the existing workforce from static role assignments into intelligent, professional specialists capable of independently reasoning, collaborating and preparing work for execution.

This sprint is about building the **brains** of the workforce.

OpenClaw remains the execution runtime and is **not** the focus of this sprint.

---

# Core Principle

NeedsOps is **not** an AI chatbot.

NeedsOps is a **digital workforce platform**.

Every Workforce Role must behave like a genuine professional.

Professional identity must be permanent.

Specialist Runs must be temporary.

OpenClaw remains only the execution layer.

---

# Architecture

The architecture after this sprint must be:

```text
Conversation
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓
Worker Profile
        ↓
Temporary Specialist Run
        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

        ↓

```

The Professional DNA layer becomes the intellectual property of NeedsOps.
