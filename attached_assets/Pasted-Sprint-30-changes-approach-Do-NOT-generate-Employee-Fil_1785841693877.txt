Sprint 30 changes approach.

Do NOT generate Employee Files yet.

Do NOT invent specialist personalities, responsibilities, authority, or professional judgement.

Instead, Sprint 30 begins with a Professional Design phase.

The objective is to capture human-defined executive and professional judgement before implementation.

The Chief of Staff and Executive Assistant remain the reference implementations.

Every remaining specialist will follow a four-stage process:

Stage 0
Professional Design Brief

↓

Stage 1
Employee File

↓

Stage 2
Implementation

↓

Stage 3
Validation & Runtime Integration

Your task is ONLY to prepare the platform for this workflow.

Review the current Employee File architecture and determine what additions are required to support a Professional Design Brief.

Design a reusable Professional Design Brief template that can be stored alongside each Employee File.

The Design Brief should NOT contain runtime prompts.

Instead it should capture the thinking behind the specialist.

Include sections such as:

• Purpose of the role
• Why the organisation hires this role
• Professional philosophy
• Daily operational priorities
• Weekly executive responsibilities
• Decision-making approach
• Professional mental models
• Common judgement calls
• Typical escalation scenarios
• Authority boundaries
• Professional risks
• Common mistakes inexperienced staff make
• Quality expectations
• Trusted information sources
• Collaboration with other specialists
• Success indicators
• Failure indicators
• Professional red flags
• Required evidence before making recommendations
• Situations where the specialist must refuse work
• Situations requiring another specialist

Review the current Employee File schema.

Recommend whether the Design Brief should:

A) become part of the Employee File

or

B) exist as a separate design artefact compiled into the Employee File.

Do not implement yet.

Produce a recommendation with reasoning.

Then design the reusable template for approval.

Stop after the template and recommendation.

Do not generate Employee Files.

Do not modify runtime code.

Do not implement specialist logic.

Wait for approval before continuing.
