NeedsOps AI+
Workforce Catalogue Streamlining — 32 Specialists to 17 AI Employees

Continue from Sprint 10 without changing the existing Professional DNA framework, dispatch engine, eligibility checks, specialist-run architecture or capability enforcement.

The purpose of this implementation is to restructure the existing specialist catalogue so it reflects a realistic digital workforce for Australian NDIS providers.

This is a catalogue and capability-remapping sprint only.

Do not generate new DNA content yet.

Do not activate OpenClaw.

Do not create new reasoning methodologies.

Core Design Principle

NeedsOps must represent realistic professional employees—not create a separate agent for every individual task.

A standalone AI employee should represent a professional role an NDIS provider could reasonably recruit.

Narrow responsibilities such as:

calendar management
communication drafting
policy drafting
corrective-action tracking
invoice review
social-media management
research
workflow design

must generally be represented as capabilities or competencies belonging to an appropriate AI employee.

The model is:

Organisation
    ↓
Department
    ↓
AI Employee
    ↓
Responsibilities and competencies
    ↓
Capabilities
    ↓
Tools and connectors
    ↓
Runtime

Do not treat every capability as an employee.
