Sprint 30.1 – Capture the Shared Professional Governance Framework

Objective

The founder workshops have now established the shared professional philosophy that governs every current and future NeedsOps AI employee.

These are no longer working notes.

They now become version-controlled constitutional artefacts that sit above every Employee File.

Do NOT modify runtime behaviour.

Do NOT generate specialist content.

Do NOT create Employee Files.

This sprint is documentation, governance and architecture only.

────────────────────────────────────────

Background

The following shared artefacts have now been approved:

1. NeedsOps Professional Constitution v1.0
2. NeedsOps Executive Operating System v1.0
3. NeedsOps Universal Decision Hierarchy v1.0

Every AI employee will inherit these before inheriting any role-specific behaviour.

These documents become the constitutional foundation of the Digital Workforce.

Deliverable 5

Governance Rules

Document the governance rules.

Examples:

• These documents are founder-approved.
• They are version controlled.
• Runtime manifests compile selected operational principles only.
• Workshop notes never become runtime instructions.
• Employee Files cannot contradict these documents.
• Specialist Design Briefs extend these documents but never override them.
• Constitutional principles override role-specific behaviour.
• Legislative obligations override organisational policy.
• Participant safety overrides operational convenience.
