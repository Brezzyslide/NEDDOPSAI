────────────────────────────────────────
SPRINT OBJECTIVE
────────────────────────────────────────

Build the AI Workforce Operations Centre.

This becomes the operational command centre where organisation owners and administrators supervise, monitor and manage their AI workforce exactly as they would a human workforce.

The experience should answer:

• What is each specialist doing?
• Are they healthy?
• Are they trained?
• Are they ready?
• What work is waiting?
• What needs my attention?

The interface should feel like managing professional employees, not AI models.

────────────────────────────────────────
PART 1
AI Workforce Dashboard
────────────────────────────────────────

Create a Workforce Operations dashboard.

Display:

• Total specialists
• Active
• Busy
• Idle
• Awaiting approval
• Suspended
• Training required
• Offline
• Average quality score
• Average confidence
• Organisation readiness score

Show live workload summary.

PART 2

────────────────────────────────────────

Display:

• Responsibilities
• Goals
• Recent completed work
• Current work
• Pending work
• Quality trend
• Confidence trend

• Organisation Library

────────────────────────────────────────
PART 3
Readiness Engine
────────────────────────────────────────

Replace generic readiness with detailed readiness analysis.

Display blockers such as:

• Required policies missing
• Required templates missing
• No approved language profile
• Knowledge requires review
• Recent regulatory changes
• Retraining recommended
• Suspended by administrator
• Missing organisation configuration

Each blocker should include:

• reason
• severity
• recommended action
• direct link to resolve
