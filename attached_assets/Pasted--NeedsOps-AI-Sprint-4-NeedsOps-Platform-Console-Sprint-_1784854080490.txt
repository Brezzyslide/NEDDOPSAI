# NeedsOps AI+

# Sprint 4 — NeedsOps Platform Console

Sprint 3 has been approved.

Proceed with Sprint 4 only.

Do not begin OpenClaw integration, browser execution, local device execution, Stripe payments or external connectors.

The objective of Sprint 4 is to build the complete NeedsOps Platform Console.

This console is used only by NeedsOps staff.

It is completely separate from customer organisation administration.

Customer organisation owners must never see this console.

---

# Vision

The Platform Console is the operational headquarters of NeedsOps AI+.

Everything required to operate the SaaS business should eventually be manageable from here without writing code.

This sprint builds Version 1.

---

# Platform Roles

Support platform roles independent of customer organisations.

Roles:

* Platform Super Admin
* Platform Operations
* Platform Billing
* Platform Support
* Platform Security
* Platform Auditor
* Platform Developer (optional)

Platform roles must never be inherited from customer organisation ownership.

All platform actions must be audited.
