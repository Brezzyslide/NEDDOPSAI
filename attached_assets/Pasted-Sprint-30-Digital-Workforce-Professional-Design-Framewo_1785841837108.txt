Sprint 30 – Digital Workforce Professional Design Framework

Change of approach.

Do NOT generate Employee Files.

Do NOT invent specialist personalities.

Do NOT invent responsibilities.

Do NOT invent authority.

Do NOT invent professional judgement.

Professional judgement will come from founder-led workshops and approved Professional Design Briefs.

Your role is to build the framework that supports this process.

──────────────────────────────────────

Background

The Chief of Staff and Executive Assistant remain the reference implementations.

Every remaining specialist will now follow the same professional development lifecycle.

Professional Design Workshop
        ↓
Professional Design Brief (Founder Approved)
        ↓
Employee File
        ↓
Capability Catalogue
        ↓
Authority Matrix
        ↓
Runtime Manifest
        ↓
Implementation
        ↓
Validation
        ↓
Production

No Employee File should be created until its Design Brief has been completed and approved.

──────────────────────────────────────

Your task

Review the current Employee File architecture.

Review:

• Employee File schema
• DNA schema
• Runtime Manifest compiler
• Worker Profile
• Capability definitions
• Responsibility definitions
• Authority definitions
• Validation logic

Design a reusable Professional Design Framework.

The framework should support every current and future specialist.

──────────────────────────────────────

Deliverable 1

Create a reusable Professional Design Brief template.

This should NOT be runtime content.

It is the constitutional design document that sits behind every Employee File.

Include sections such as:

1. Why this role exists
2. Organisational purpose
3. Problems this role solves
4. Professional philosophy
5. Executive mindset
6. Leadership style
7. Daily priorities
8. Weekly priorities
9. Monthly responsibilities
10. Professional mental models
11. Common judgement calls
12. Decision-making framework
13. Professional risks
14. Typical mistakes inexperienced people make
15. Escalation philosophy
16. Success indicators
17. Failure indicators
18. Professional red flags
19. Evidence required before recommendations
20. Situations requiring refusal
21. Situations requiring escalation
22. Trusted information sources
23. Collaboration expectations
24. Regulatory considerations
25. Ethics considerations
26. Professional limitations

This template must be reusable for every specialist.

──────────────────────────────────────

Rules

Do NOT generate Employee Files.

Do NOT generate specialist content.

Do NOT invent personalities.

Do NOT modify runtime behaviour.

Do NOT implement specialist logic.

Do NOT generate prompts.

Produce only the reusable framework.

After the framework is complete, stop and wait for approval before beginning the first specialist workshop.

The goal is to ensure every NeedsOps specialist is built from founder-approved professional knowledge rather than AI-generated assumptions.
